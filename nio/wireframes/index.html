  1. 1Status bar. Gear indicator (P R N D, active gear highlighted) and speed sit at the driver's side. Time centred. NOMI mic, signal, range on the right.
  2. 2Next turn. Biggest text on screen while driving: distance + street. Readable in one glance.

  4. 4Map controls. Search, layers, voice guidance, re-centre. 64 pt targets on the edge furthest from the driver's hand path.

  6. 6Next event. Ties the drive to Emma's calendar: the reason for this trip.
  7. 7Dock. Climate at both corners (driver/passenger), six app destinations. Selected item = filled icon + tinted pill, no glow.

  1. 1Edit bar. Size (2/3·1/3 or 1/2·1/2), swap sides, fold the context pane, Done. Two fixed sizes instead of a free drag: faster and harder to get wrong.
  2. 2Context pane, swapped to the left. Its cards can be reordered with the grips, or removed; Add card brings back hidden ones.

  4. 4Dock dimmed. Dock and status bar are fixed and can't be edited, so they fade back while editing. Shifting into D exits edit mode automatically.
