  1. 1Status bar. Gear indicator (P R N D, active gear highlighted), drive-mode chip (tap for a quick picker) and speed sit at the driver's side. Time centred. Signal and range on the right (NOMI moved to the dock).
  2. 2Next turn. Biggest text on screen while driving: distance + street. Readable in one glance.
  3. 3Trip bar. Arrival, time and distance left, End route. Tabular numbers so values don't jump.
  4. 4Map controls. Search, layers, voice guidance, re-centre. 64 pt targets on the edge furthest from the driver's hand path.
  5. 5Context pane: Now playing. Album art placeholder named by slot. Three large controls only while driving.
  6. 6Trip. Distance driven, average consumption and drive time, tied to the reason for the trip (the 14:00 Mudam visit). Battery energy stays on the Car screen, so nothing is shown twice.
  7. 7Dock. Climate at both corners, six app destinations, a permanent NOMI button and one customisable shortcut slot (dashed; here Seat heat with level bars). Selected item = filled icon + tinted pill, no glow.
  8. 8View switch. Calm (essentials only), Map, Car: changes what both panes show. Borrowed from the Polestar Formula 2030.
